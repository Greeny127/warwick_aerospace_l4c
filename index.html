Warwick builds rockets.

We are the rocketry division of Warwick Aerospace, at the University of Warwick. Our rockets have gone higher each time: from the Hummingbird, a thrust control system, at 3 m in 2014, to WR-1 at 1 km, to MACH-25, a two-stage payload, at 2.5 km in 2024/25. This year we are entering Launch 4 Change, a national competition between 10 universities, organised by Durham University and funded by ARIA. Our entry, L4CRocket, is aimed at 15,000 feet and will carry a climate payload. Four teams are building it together: airframe, modelling and simulation, the climate payload, and electric vehicle integration.

Four teams. One rocket.
