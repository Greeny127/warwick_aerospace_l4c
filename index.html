Reaching 15,000 feet.

Warwick builds rockets.

We are the rocketry division of Warwick Aerospace, at the University of Warwick. Our rockets have gone higher each time: MACH-25 flew to 153 m on smaller motors and NRC reached 540 m in 2024/25, then MACH-26, a two-stage payload, reached 2.5 km in 2025/26. This year we are entering Launch 4 Change, a national competition between 12 universities, organised by Durham University and funded by ARIA. Our entry, L4CRocket, is aimed at 15,000 feet and will carry a climate payload. Four teams are building it together: airframe, modelling and simulation, the climate payload, and electric vehicle integration.

Launch 4
Change.

Launch 4 Change is an outreach and innovation programme funded by the Advanced Research + Invention Agency (ARIA). It supports university rocketry societies in two ways: helping them design and launch climate-monitoring payloads, which are instruments that collect data in the atmosphere, and helping them mentor young people taking part in the UK Rocketry Challenge (UKROC). The programme broadens participation and helps create the next generation of scientists, engineers and climate leaders.
